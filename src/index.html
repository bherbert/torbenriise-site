About

A scientist who became a storyteller

Torben Riise was born and raised in Copenhagen, Denmark, where he earned a PhD in biotechnology and an MBA in business economics. He then spent 25 years as an executive at international biotech companies, working with enzymes, hormones, gene editing and stem cells.

Alongside that career he wrote hundreds of freelance articles for Danish and American publications on business, social issues, the ethics of technology and the future. Today he is the author of nine novels and four non-fiction books, with more in progress.
